# Automatric Design Direction (DESIGN.md)

> Brand identity, craftsmanship standards, and visual architecture for Automatric: AI Growth Agency.

## 1. Identity & Character
- **Product**: Automatric (Agensi Pertumbuhan Bisnis Berbasis AI)
- **Target Audience**: Pemilik bisnis UMKM, brand retail, dan entrepreneur Indonesia yang membutuhkan automasi cepat tanpa tim teknis rumit.
- **Brand Personality**: Presisi, cepat (<24 jam turnaround), transparan (sekali bayar), dan berorientasi hasil nyata.
- **Design Read**: High-craft editorial dark landing page for modern Indonesian businesses, dial **ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 1**.

## 2. Antislop Dials
- **ENERGY**: `2` (Balanced, high contrast, clean focal points, stark white typography on deep obsidian).
- **RHYTHM**: `3` (Dynamic editorial rhythm: Asymmetric Hero with Live Proof Console -> Comparison Split Section -> Interactive Product Simulator -> Structured 4-Phase Roadmap -> Interactive Choose Plan Matrix -> Minimal Accordion FAQ).
- **MOTION**: `1` (Hover transitions and state toggles only. No scroll-reveal or entrance animations.)

## 3. Strict Color System (Anti-Rainbow Rule)
- **Core Canvas**: Obsidian Black (`#050505`).
- **Elevated Surfaces**: Deep Neutral Mattes (`#08080a`, `#0b0b0e`, `#101014`, `#16161c`).
- **Hairline Borders**: Crisp Subtle Dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Typography**: Stark White (`#ffffff`) for titles, Muted Zinc (`#a1a1aa`, `#71717a`) for body, JetBrains Mono for metadata and operational tags.
- **Single Intentional Accent**: Electric Emerald (`#10b981`, `#34d399`) reserved exclusively for active states, interactive selection, and verification checks.
- **EXPLICIT BANS**:
  - No rainbow or cyan/purple gradient text.
  - No background blueprint grid patterns (`.bg-grid-pattern`).
  - No colored radial blur halos behind content.
  - No neon cyan or purple glow effects.

## 4. Architectural & Craftsmanship Standards
- **Contrast Standard**: All color combinations verified with `contrast-check.py` meeting WCAG AA (minimum 7.95:1 to 20.38:1 contrast ratios).
- **Mobile Usability**: All interactive buttons, tabs, and links maintain minimum 44px tap targets.
- **Interactivity**: Every interactive element has real behavior (Live WhatsApp simulator, Prompt-to-Banner switcher, Ads case study toggle, and Choose Plan WhatsApp message generator).
- **No Em Dashes**: Zero em dashes (`—`) across all UI and documentation copy.
