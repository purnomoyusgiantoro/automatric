# Automatric Design Direction (DESIGN.md)

> Brand identity and visual system for Automatric: AI Growth Agency.

## 1. Identity & Character
- **Product**: Automatric (Agensi Pertumbuhan Bisnis Berbasis AI)
- **Target Audience**: Pemilik bisnis UMKM, brand retail, dan entrepreneur Indonesia yang membutuhkan automasi cepat tanpa tim teknis rumit.
- **Brand Personality**: Presisi, cepat (<24 jam turnaround), transparan (sekali bayar), dan berorientasi hasil nyata.
- **Design Read**: Growth Agency landing page for modern Indonesian businesses & retail, in an ultra-dark glassmorphism & emerald precision style, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

## 2. Antislop Dials
- **ENERGY**: `2` (Balanced, high contrast, clean focal points)
- **RHYTHM**: `2` (Consistent section structure with content-driven variations: interactive WhatsApp chat simulator, multi-format design pipeline, and verified ads dashboard)
- **MOTION**: `2` (Smooth transitions, state reveals, interactive card selection, without chaotic endless loops)

## 3. Color System
- **Core Neutral Base**: Deep Obsidian Black (`#030508`), Dark Slate Navy (`#070b14`), Card Surface (`#090d16`)
- **Text & Borders**: High-contrast Slate (`#f8fafc` headings, `#94a3b8` body, `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` borders)
- **Single Intentional Accent**: Emerald (`#10b981`, `#059669`) reserved for primary action points, selected states, and confirmed operational badges.

## 4. Typography
- **Headings & Primary UI**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Clean, modern geometric sans with high legibility)
- **Metrics & Code**: JetBrains Mono (Used selectively for numbers, turnaround indicators, and telemetry identifiers)

## 5. Architectural Standards
- WCAG AA compliant contrast ratio (4.5:1+ for body text).
- Glassmorphism capped at 1-2 focal elements (Navbar + Selected Plan Confirmation Bar); cards use solid matte surfaces.
- Zero decorative emojis in structural UI components.
- Zero em dashes (`—`) across all copy.
