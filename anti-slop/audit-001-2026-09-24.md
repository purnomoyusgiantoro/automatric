# Anti-Slop Audit Report: Automatric Landing Page

- **Audit ID**: `audit-001-2026-09-24`
- **Target**: `D:\Documents\automatric`
- **Inspector**: Antislop AI Filter & Antigravity Agent
- **Design Read**: Growth Agency landing page for modern Indonesian businesses & retail, in an ultra-dark glassmorphism & emerald precision style, dial `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2`.

---

## 1. Numbered Findings & Resolution

### Finding #1 [HIGH / Hard Gate] - R-02 (Copywriting: Em Dash Ban)
- **Violation**: The em dash character (`—`) appeared in `index.html` (line 7), `PricingSection.tsx` (lines 181, 209), and en-dashes (`–`) appeared in timeframes (`3–7 Hari`, `3–5 Hari`).
- **Fix**: Replaced all em dashes with colons (`:`) or parentheses `()`, and normalized range characters to standard ASCII hyphens (`-`).
- **Status**: RESOLVED (PASS).

### Finding #2 [HIGH / Hard Gate] - R-24 & R-26 (Dead Navigation Link)
- **Violation**: Navbar contained `{ name: "Keunggulan", href: "#keunggulan" }`, but no corresponding `<section id="keunggulan">` existed in the layout.
- **Fix**: Removed `#keunggulan` from `siteConfig.navLinks`, retaining strictly real existing anchors (`#layanan`, `#cara-kerja`, `#pricing`, `#faq`).
- **Status**: RESOLVED (PASS).

### Finding #3 [HIGH / Hard Gate] - R-37 (Design Direction Required)
- **Violation**: No explicit `DESIGN.md` existed defining brand identity, mood, and dials.
- **Fix**: Created `DESIGN.md` specifying target audience, color tokens (Obsidian base + Emerald single accent), typography, and dials `ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2`.
- **Status**: RESOLVED (PASS).

### Finding #4 [MEDIUM / Purpose-Gate] - R-04 & R-16 (Generic AI Icons & Buzzwords)
- **Violation**:
  - `Sparkles` icon was imported and used generically in Navbar logo, Pillars section header, Pricing featured badge, and Hero tag.
  - Literal emojis (`⚡`, `🤖`, `📈`, `💰`) were used inside hero feature pill badges.
  - Buzzword "Next-Gen" was used in taglines without concrete meaning.
- **Fix**:
  - Replaced generic `Sparkles` with semantic icons (`Layers`, `Flame`).
  - Removed all decorative emojis from feature badges, using clean SVG icons only.
  - Refined tagline to "Agensi Pertumbuhan Bisnis Berbasis AI" and hero eyebrow to concrete slot availability.
- **Status**: RESOLVED (PASS).

### Finding #5 [MEDIUM / Purpose-Gate] - R-10 & R-13 (Excessive Glassmorphism & Multi-Glow)
- **Violation**: Glassmorphism blur and colored glows were stacked across hero background, all 3 pricing cards, and metrics ticker simultaneously.
- **Fix**:
  - Maintained matte solid dark backgrounds (`bg-[#080c16]` and `bg-[#0b101c]`) on cards.
  - Reserved emerald glow exclusively for the active selected pricing card (`isSelected`).
  - Capped frosted glassmorphism to 2 focal elements: sticky header Navbar and dynamic confirmation bar.
- **Status**: RESOLVED (PASS).

### Finding #6 [HIGH / Hard Gate] - R-17 & R-36 (Data Transparency & Evidence)
- **Violation**: Ads dashboard metrics in Pillar 3 could be mistaken for global unverified agency claims.
- **Fix**: Explicitly added transparency label `[Simulasi Studi Kasus Retail UMKM Fashion]` so the user clearly understands it is an operational simulation model.
- **Status**: RESOLVED (PASS).
